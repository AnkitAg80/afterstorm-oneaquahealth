# AfterStorm — 4:15 demo script

Record with a clean browser window and the latest committed data. Open the local page or approved public demo at `?city=CO&mode=replay&visits=5&guide=0#/`; prepare [ServiceRequest/1046](https://sandbox.hl7europe.eu/oneaquahealth/fhir/ServiceRequest/1046) in another tab. The `?guide=0` shortcut is only for recording. Do not refresh the forecast just before recording unless you will read the new card. This script targets **3:45–4:30** with deliberate time to click and show each state. Avoid speaking faster to fit extra features.

| Time | Screen action | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0:00–0:20** | Start on Coimbra **Overview**, replay banner visible. | “At the Paris 2024 Olympics, rain still decided whether athletes could swim in the Seine. Here are 96 public OneAquaHealth lab records; 95 are from 2023. This is a replay of a real Coimbra storm on 10 May 2026.” |
| **0:20–0:50** | Point to historical evidence and the city/mode controls. | “This is our Track 6 entry: early warning and resilience planning. Old faecal, pathogen and antibiotic-resistance categories connect stream health with people and pets, but they cannot tell us today's water quality. A coordinator needs to decide where to sample next.” |
| **0:50–1:40** | Point to rain value, dates, visits ring, and three steps in the hero. Open **Evidence & data** briefly if room. | “Every Coimbra site recorded at least 20 millimetres on the archived day. That matches the WMO/ETCCDI R20mm definition of a very heavy precipitation day; here it is only a prototype trigger. AfterStorm proposes the next two **calendar days** for reassessment at five streams. The window is experimental; a coordinator confirms it and the lab team chooses the tests.” |
| **1:40–2:15** | On Overview, show **Protection check**. Press **Show a budget that covers every notice site (8 visits)**, then return budget to 5. | “With five trips, draft-notice sites C2 and C16 remain outside the visit budget. The protection check makes that trade-off visible. Eight trips would cover every notice site; this button changes capacity, not the rank.” |
| **2:15–2:40** | Go to **Notices**; open `#notice-C16` if quicker. Show urban setting and confirmation date. | “These are drafts, never automatic warnings. C16 shows the stored urban context; density is a proxy, not a head count. The coordinator can plan a sample to confirm when an incident has ended, then decide whether to lift the notice.” |
| **2:40–3:05** | Go to **Plan & map**; open `#card-C5`. Show Portuguese safety line, q6 dead animals and q7 unusual colour. | “Citizens get a safe, bank-side card in the city's language. They can record people-and-pets contact, dead animals and ecosystem signs, then make the full record in the official OneAquaHealth app. These answers do not change the plan.” |
| **3:05–3:30** | Approve C5. Show Review export/import controls and stale chip with a prepared tampered review file if available. Download **Lab sheet (CSV)** and open it in Excel for about 3 seconds. | “A coordinator approves or holds each visit. The approval is tied to the evidence: when that evidence changes, it asks for review again. This file shares the review with a colleague; the lab sheet hands the exact current visits to a lab. Nothing is uploaded.” |
| **3:30–3:50** | Show sandbox **ServiceRequest/1046** for at least 10 seconds with URL visible. | “Five replay requests were filed once as draft proposals, 1046 through 1050, with the earlier resource shape. Today's v2 export passed the core R4 validator with zero errors; the OneAquaHealth profile could not be checked. The download button does not send it.” |
| **3:50–4:15** | Click **See latest forecast →**. Read the actual city, fetched-at time, rainfall, and visit count currently shown. Finish on Overview or Evidence & data. | “LIVE uses the latest **saved** forecast, fetched at the time shown. This card says [read the current forecast and visit count]. It is a sampling proposal, not a contamination prediction or water-safety clearance. From streams to systems.” |

If the weather card differs from this script, **read the card**, including a no-storm or incomplete-data state. For the stale-review shot, use a local file whose C5 signature differs; do not claim that changing a citizen answer invalidates a review. Never show a v2 file as if it were the sandbox's v1 record.

Source for the opening Paris line: [Olympic Library analysis of Paris 2024](https://library.olympics.com/digitalCollection/DigitalCollectionAttachmentDownloadHandler.ashx?documentId=3416736&parentDocumentId=3416735&skipCopyright=true&skipWatermark=true). The R20mm basis is the [ETCCDI index list](https://etccdi.pacificclimate.org/list_27_indices.shtml).
